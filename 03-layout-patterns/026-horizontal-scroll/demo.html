100 years of trams

City Transport Museum, Hall 3. Scroll sideways through a century of the red trams that shaped our streets.

1926

The first electric line

Line 1 opens between the station and the cathedral. Twelve wooden cars, open at the ends.

1938

The famous red

The city paints every tram bright red so they can be seen in winter fog. The color stays.

1944

Trams in wartime

With no fuel for buses, trams carry the whole city. Women drive most of them.

1968

Almost closed

Council plans to replace trams with buses. 40,000 people sign a petition to keep them.

1995

Low floor cars

New trams with no steps make travel easier for wheelchairs and prams.

2026

A century on

Six lines, 94 trams and 48 million trips a year. Still red.
